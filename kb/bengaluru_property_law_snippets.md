# Bengaluru Property Law — Quick Reference Snippets

## 1. Stamp Duty Rates (Karnataka, current)
- Residential property ≤ ₹20 lakh: 2%
- Residential property ₹20L–₹45L: 3%
- Residential property > ₹45 lakh: 5%
- Additional surcharge: 10% on stamp duty (for BBMP limits)
- Registration fee: 1% (max ₹15,000 for residential < ₹20L)

## 2. Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
- Issued by Sub-Registrar's Office.
- Shows all registered transactions (mortgages, liens, sales) for a property over a given period.
- EC with zero encumbrances for 13–30 years is generally considered clean.
- Form 15 = no encumbrances; Form 16 = encumbrances exist.

## 3. BBMP Khata Mutation Process
- Submit application at e-Aasthi portal (https://bbmpeaasthi.karnataka.gov.in) or ward office.
- Documents needed: Registered sale deed, previous Khata extract, EC for 12 years, latest property tax receipt, identity proof.
- Timeline: ~30–60 working days (BBMP target).

## 4. Conversion Certificate (Section 95, KLR Act)
- Required for agricultural land converted to non-agricultural use.
- Without conversion certificate, apartment/villa built on such land cannot get legal sanction.
- Check Form 9, 11A (revenue records) alongside Mother Deed.

## 5. RERA Registration (Karnataka)
- Projects with > 8 apartments or > 500 sq m area must be registered with K-RERA.
- Buyers can verify at https://rera.karnataka.gov.in
- RERA registration number must appear in sale agreement and deed.

## 6. Karnataka Land Reforms Act — Restrictions
- Agricultural land cannot be purchased by non-agriculturists without government permission.
- Section 79A/79B imposes ceiling on agricultural land holdings.
- Converts must show Section 95 order before land sale.

## 7. Patta vs Khata
- Patta = revenue record (taluk office) for rural/peri-urban property.
- Khata = BBMP municipal record for urban Bengaluru property.
- Some properties on Bengaluru's periphery may show Patta instead of Khata — verify jurisdiction.
