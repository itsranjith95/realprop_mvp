# Mother Deed — Karnataka / Bengaluru Rules & Requirements

## What is a Mother Deed?
A Mother Deed (also called a Title Deed or Sale Deed) is the primary document that establishes ownership of immovable property. It traces the chain of title from the original grant to the current owner.

## Mandatory Fields (Bengaluru/Karnataka)
- **Seller name**: Full legal name of the transferor (must match Khata owner for continuity).
- **Buyer name**: Full legal name of the transferee; becomes the new Khata holder.
- **Property description**: Survey number, site number, area (in sq ft or sq m), locality, ward.
- **Registration date**: Date of execution and sub-registrar registration.
- **Document/Registration number**: Assigned by the Sub-Registrar's Office (SRO).
- **Consideration amount**: Sale price in INR.
- **Stamp duty & registration fee**: Proof of payment required under Karnataka Stamp Act.

## Title Chain Continuity
- Each Mother Deed must show a clear chain: seller of the current deed should have been the buyer in the previous deed.
- Any break in the chain (unknown grantor, missing intermediate deed) is a red flag.
- Karnataka Registration Act requires registration of all sale deeds above ₹100.

## Common Red Flags
1. Name discrepancy between seller/buyer in deed and Khata owner.
2. Property ID or survey number mismatch across documents.
3. Missing sub-registrar stamp or registration number.
4. Deed not registered within 4 months of execution (Section 23, Registration Act).
5. Encumbrance Certificate (EC) shows mortgage or lien not mentioned in deed.

## Applicable Law
- Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957
- Registration Act, 1908 (as applicable to Karnataka)
- Transfer of Property Act, 1882
