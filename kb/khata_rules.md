# Khata — Karnataka / Bengaluru Rules & Requirements

## What is a Khata?
A Khata is a revenue/municipal document issued by BBMP (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike) or the relevant local body (CMC/TMC) that records a property for tax assessment purposes. It is NOT a title document but is essential for obtaining building plans, loans, and utility connections.

## Types of Khata
- **A-Khata**: Property is listed in BBMP's official register. Required for bank loans and building plans.
- **B-Khata**: Property is in BBMP limits but not fully approved (e.g., revenue/BDA conversion pending). Higher risk.

## Mandatory Fields
- **Owner name**: Must match the buyer in the most recent registered sale deed (Mother Deed).
- **Khata number**: Unique assessment number assigned by BBMP.
- **Property ID (PID)**: BBMP's property identification number.
- **Ward number and zone**: BBMP ward jurisdiction.
- **Property area and usage**: Residential/commercial, built-up area.
- **Assessment date**: Date Khata was issued or last assessed.

## Khata Transfer Requirements (Bengaluru)
When a property changes hands:
1. Apply to BBMP with: registered sale deed, previous Khata, encumbrance certificate (EC), tax receipts.
2. BBMP verifies and issues new Khata in buyer's name.
3. Failure to transfer Khata creates name mismatch — owner in deed ≠ owner in Khata.

## Common Red Flags
1. **Name mismatch**: Khata owner ≠ Mother Deed buyer — title continuity broken.
2. **B-Khata**: Signals revenue conversion or layout approval is pending.
3. **Outdated Khata**: Not updated after recent sale.
4. **Multiple Khatas for same property**: Possible fraud or survey discrepancy.
5. **No Khata at all**: Property may not be regularized with BBMP.

## Applicable Authority
- BBMP (Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike) for Bengaluru city limits
- CMC/TMC for properties in Bengaluru urban agglomeration
- Revenue Department for agricultural / conversion land
